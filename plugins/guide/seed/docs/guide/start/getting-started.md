# Getting started

This app keeps your organisation's work in one place. After you sign in you land on
**Home**, and everything else is one click away in the navigation on the left. This guide explains
each part, and **Ask the Guide** answers questions about it in plain words.

## How it is laid out

- **Home** is where you start: shortcuts to the things you use most.
- **The navigation** on the left groups the screens. What you see in it depends on your role: an
  admin also sees **Settings** and **Activity**.
- **The top bar** holds your notifications, your account menu and the **?** button.

## Do it

1. Open a screen from the navigation on the left.
2. Press <kbd>?</kbd> on any screen (or click the **?** button in the top bar) to ask the guide a
   question without leaving the page.
3. Open **User guide** in the navigation to read it page by page, or search it from the box
   above the contents.

> **RULE OF THUMB** — Ask the Guide first when a screen is unfamiliar. Every answer links to the
> page it came from, so you can read the whole story when you need it.

## Watch out for

- **A screen you expected is missing.** Your role decides what you can open. Ask an admin of your
  organisation if you need more.
- **The answer does not cover your question.** The guide only knows how the app works, not your
  organisation's own data. Say what you were trying to do, and it will point you to the closest
  page.
